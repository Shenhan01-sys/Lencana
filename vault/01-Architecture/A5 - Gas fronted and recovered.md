# A5 - Gas fronted and recovered

**Part of:** [[01-Architecture/01 - Architecture]]
**Source:** `contracts/SettlementSplit.sol:112` (`sharesOf`) · `signer/src/delegation.js:2`

**Summary:** The lookup table for the money-of-gas question: three different actors are involved in one issuance and they must not be merged in the reader's head — the agent **signs**, the platform **broadcasts**, the platform's **fee share** is what makes it whole. This note records the measured cost scale of the two flows, the recovery mechanism as it is actually implemented in `SettlementSplit` (a fixed bps share, hard-capped, one-way), and the one accounting rule the design deliberately does not break: **gas belongs to the transaction, and whoever broadcasts it pays it — it is never subtracted from the issuer's share as a percentage line item.** The hub essay carries the reasoning and the framing discipline; this carries the numbers and the guards.

**Key points:**
- **Signing ≠ broadcasting ≠ paying.** `attest()` would record `msg.sender` as the attester, so a platform that simply sent it would *become* the issuer. The route used is `attestByDelegation`, where the attester is the EIP-712 **signer** (`signer/src/delegation.js:2-8`); `onAttest` gates on `_issuer[attestation.attester]`, not `msg.sender` (`contracts/CredentialResolver.sol:243`), which is the only reason a third-party agent can be represented by a relayer at all.
- **Zero BNB on the agent's side is measured, not asserted:** `signer/scripts/delegate.js:201` requires the agent's balance to be unchanged, and `:87` prints which address paid. Cost scale from the runs recorded in `README.md:52`: one delegated attestation 370,131 gas; three lesson credentials in **one** batched transaction 1,024,813 gas; a non-delegated attestation via `signer/scripts/issue.js` 316,384 gas (`README.md:56`).
- **Recovery is a fixed bps share of the fee, not a per-transaction reimbursement.** `sharesOf(amount)` has exactly two terms: `platformShare = (amount * platformBps) / BPS_DENOM` and `issuerShare = amount - platformShare` (`contracts/SettlementSplit.sol:113-114`, `BPS_DENOM = 10000` at `:77`). The cap is `MAX_BPS = 2500` — 25%, enforced at construction with `BpsTooHigh(proposed, maximum)` (`:76`, `:54`), and `platformBps` may only fall: `decreasePlatformBps()` reverts `BpsMayOnlyDecrease` when `next >= platformBps`, so even an unchanged value is refused (`:183-184`). Raising the share means deploying a new contract, visible in the address → **D38**.
- **The deployed rates were re-read from chain 97 on 2026-09-25**, not taken from this note: `cast call 0xcB00E62B888113A1B09Fe9bbd01afC946e8e1bBE "platformBps()(uint16)" --rpc-url https://bsc-testnet.publicnode.com` → `1000` (10%), and the same command with `"MAX_BPS()(uint16)"` → `2500`.
- **Cost scale of the paid route:** one settlement is 114,728 gas (`settleWithPermit`) + 75,931 gas (`splitErc20`) = **190,659 gas**, read from the two receipts (`README.md:55`; echoed as `PER_VERIFICATION_GAS = 190659` in `signer/src/server.js:230`). The server's price is `BigInt(process.env.X402_PRICE ?? '1000')` atomic units of a 6-decimal demo token (`signer/src/server.js:39`, `contracts/DemoCourseToken.sol:29`).
- **The arithmetic that forced batch pricing** (`signer/src/server.js:223-228`, **D40**): the 190,659 gas is *fixed per settlement* — it does not scale with how many credentials the response carries. One report per payment therefore charges 190,659 gas to one report; a full batch of `BATCH_MAX = 25` (`:229`) charges 190,659 / 25 ≈ 7,626 gas per report (arithmetic on the two cited figures, not a separate measurement). Without the batch, the platform's own 10% is eaten by the gas it fronted.
- **The accounting rule.** No code path moves BNB out of the split: `splitErc20` reads **its own token balance** and never touches native value (`contracts/SettlementSplit.sol:136`, `uint256 held = token.balanceOf(address(this))`), so the BNB for the two settlement transactions leaves a different wallet entirely — the facilitator's (`signer/src/x402.js:180-215`, and `README.md:54`: the demo script's 2,806,675 gas was *paid by the platform*). The harness asserts the consequence rather than the intention: `payeeGain + platformGain === price` exactly (`signer/scripts/x402-check.js:158`), the client's token balance drops by exactly the price (`:160`), and the split holds nothing afterwards (`:161`). If gas were netted from the issuer, that first equality could not hold.

**Detail:**
- **Where the rule bites when you change code.** Adding a "gas reimbursement" term inside `sharesOf()` would make it a *third* share of the payment and would break the equality `x402-check.js:158` exists to enforce. Gas-side changes belong in the broadcaster's accounting (its own receipts), never in the payment split. Same reasoning as the platform's other one-way lever, `delistIssuer` → [[Concepts/Issuer Delisted vs Revoked]]: a human input may only make things stricter for someone else.
- **Do not convert the fee into money here.** The paid route is denominated in `Lencana Demo Coin`, which has an open `mint()` and no market (`contracts/DemoCourseToken.sol:30-32`); the only unit that is a fact is the gas count. The dollar framing already recorded under **D40** in [[00-Overview/03 - Decisions]] is the place that comparison lives — restate it with its date, or not at all.
- **Limits of fronting, all three still true:** the relayer can *delay or drop* a delegation but cannot forge or alter one (it is signed); a leaked delegation is a bearer instrument, damage limited to *when*, nonce single-use, so `deadline` must be short — `signer/scripts/delegate.js` defaults to 900 s and warns about `0`, which means "never expires"; and an agent can always relay its own `attest()`, so the censorship is not total. `increaseNonce(newNonce)` is the agent's escape hatch and invalidates every unused delegation below it.
- **The third axis, found by measuring:** minting the artifact is a *separate* platform transaction with its own gas — `SoulboundCert.mint()` admits exactly one caller, `owner()` (`contracts/SoulboundCert.sol:107-108`). The agent's claim is the attestation; the NFT is the platform's presentation of it → [[02-Contracts/01 - Contracts]].
- **Framing discipline:** to an agent, "you never touch gas" is literally true and is the selling point. In writing, do not hide the recovery either — "the platform fronts issuance gas and recovers it through a fixed share of x402 fees" is defensible precisely because `platformBps()` can be read from the contract. **Softening the wording, not the numbers.**
- Not to be confused with the payment side of the same rail, where the *client* also sends zero transactions for a different reason (EIP-2612 `permit` instead of `approve`) → [[Concepts/Fronted Gas]].
