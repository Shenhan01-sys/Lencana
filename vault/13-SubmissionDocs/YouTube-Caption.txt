Lencana — learning credentials anyone can check for themselves (BNB Chain testnet)

=== DESKRIPSI (tempel apa adanya) ===
A certificate is just a file. Anyone can edit it. Lencana turns a finished micro-course into a credential anyone can check for themselves, without taking our word for it.

What you see in the video (screens from the live testnet app):
• Sign in with just your email: a wallet is created for you.
• Pay for a course by signing, with no gas fees (a test token on BNB Chain testnet).
• Quizzes are graded by the publisher's server. Essays go to AI grading agents, each with its own on-chain identity (ERC-8004); an agent's score only counts once a second reviewer, appointed by the publisher, approves it.
• Pass, and your credential is signed by the publisher and anchored on BNB Chain.
• Mint its soulbound badge (ERC-5192): no one can sell, transfer or burn it, and it is only minted for a valid credential.
• Anyone can verify it from a single link, with no wallet and no login. If it is ever revoked, everyone sees it.
• The credential passes the 1EdTech Open Badges 3.0 validator with zero errors.
• Behind it, one transparent pipeline: from enrollment to payout, every payment is settled on-chain.

• Cetak badge soulbound-nya (ERC-5192): tidak bisa dijual, dipindahkan, atau dibakar, dan hanya dicetak untuk kredensial yang sah.
• Siapa pun bisa memverifikasi dari satu tautan, tanpa dompet dan tanpa login. Kalau dicabut, semua orang melihatnya.
• Kredensialnya lolos validator 1EdTech Open Badges 3.0 dengan nol error.
• Di baliknya satu alur yang transparan: dari pendaftaran sampai pembayaran, setiap pembayaran diselesaikan on-chain.
