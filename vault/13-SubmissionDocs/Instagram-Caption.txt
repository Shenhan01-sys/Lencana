SHOW TIME. 🏅

Lencana is officially in the Indonesia Web3 Hackathon 2026 (Consumer Apps track), built on BNB Chain 🚀

A certificate is just a file. Anyone can edit the name or the grade, and the fake looks exactly like the real one. So we built Lencana: learning credentials anyone can check for themselves, without taking our word for it.

What's already live:
✅ Sign in with just your email, your wallet is created for you
✅ Pay for a course by signing, no gas fees
✅ Essays graded by AI agents, counted only after a second reviewer appointed by the publisher approves
✅ Credentials signed by the publisher and anchored on BNB Chain
✅ Soulbound badge (ERC-5192): no one can sell, transfer or burn it
✅ Verify from one link, no wallet, no login
✅ Passes the 1EdTech Open Badges 3.0 validator with 0 errors
