Today, a digital course certificate is usually just a PDF—and a PDF proves absolutely nothing on its own. To verify it, an employer or recruiter has to manually track down the school, ask for confirmation, and just blindly trust the answer. This makeshift system completely shuts out small, honest educational publishers who lack massive brand recognition, while allowing louder, less credible players to put whatever they want on paper. 

The underlying issue goes even deeper. A student's entire academic record—rubrics, weights, and grades—sits silently inside the issuer's private database. This data can be altered behind closed doors at any time, leaving no trace on the certificate itself. Worse yet, the student owns nothing in this deal. If the school's website crashes or the platform goes out of business, the proof of their hard work vanishes instantly. Even existing blockchain solutions get it wrong, forcing non-technical users to deal with crypto wallets and gas fees just to hold a piece of paper. 

We are not here to fix course quality; we are here to fix the proof layer. We are building a system where the grading rules are permanently sealed together with the student's actual results. A system where credentials can be instantly verified through a transparent public record, completely free of charge, and without requiring anyone to create an account.
